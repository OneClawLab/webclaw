# Why I initiated Eidux?

ChatGPT is so good but not good enough: lack synergy with my personal notes system.
- You can't easily save chat outcome to personal notes.
- You can't easily reference personal notes in chat.

Evernote is good but evolves toward a mess when you have so many notes (I have 2000 notes, mostly hand-made, not just a simple web clip).

Most AI system are invented to help people do ordinary boring tasks. 
Though it is big big help, but not enough.

There should be some AI system help people thinking deep, study continuously and systematically.
These all need a loop of conversational chat and structural summarizing / compacting what you learned.

That's why Eidux try to fill up.
